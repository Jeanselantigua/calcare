Savings & assistance

Tax tools and local programs that can lower what you pay. CalCare estimates are educational—confirm details with a tax professional or agency.

Dependent Care FSA

Set aside up to $5,000 pre-tax per year ($2,500 if married filing separately) for eligible childcare if your employer offers an FSA. Our calculator estimates savings using a simplified marginal rate from your income range.

Child & Dependent Care Tax Credit

A credit on a percentage of eligible expenses—up to $3,000 for one child or $6,000 for two or more. The percentage depends on income. FSA contributions and the credit interact; the calculator models a simple non-double-counting estimate.
