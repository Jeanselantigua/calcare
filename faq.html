Frequently asked questions

Are these prices exact?

No. Every figure is an estimate based on national baselines, a ZIP regional multiplier, care type, ages, and hours. Always confirm with providers and a tax professional.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. The basic calculator is always free. There are no subscriptions. We plan revenue from provider partnerships, donations, and possibly ads.

What is the HHS affordability index?

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services has used 7% of household income as a benchmark for affordable childcare. CalCare shows your estimated out-of-pocket cost as a share of income against that benchmark.

How do tax savings estimates work?

We model Dependent Care FSA savings with a simplified marginal rate and the Child & Dependent Care Tax Credit with simplified income-based percentages and expense caps. Real returns depend on filing status and other factors.

What data do you collect?

We collect only what’s needed to estimate costs. Calculator inputs stay in your browser unless you submit a contact request. See the privacy note below.

Who are your partners?

We’re building relationships with organizations such as FIRST 5 Santa Clara County and Choices for Children, plus childcare facilities that want to reach informed parents.
